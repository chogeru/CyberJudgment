Due to the nature of this particular track (very quiet intro), in order to make a seamless loop two separate tracks were needed to create the loop.
The Track labeled "intro" is the exact same track as "loop" except the "loop" track has a reverb tail at the beginning that is very noticeable when it is played first.
So, an intro track was necessary that excludes the noticeable tail.  

To loop this track, simply:

Make sure the "intro" track is played first, once this track is completed, immediately play the "loop" track which can then be looped repeatedly.
